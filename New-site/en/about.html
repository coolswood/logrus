About Us

Cultural Non-profit Association Logrus ry (Helsinki)

Logrus ry is a registered non-profit cultural association (rekisteröity yhdistys, Business ID: 2040144-2), founded in Helsinki in 2006. For 20 years, the association has continuously advanced independent performing arts, theatre pedagogy, and intercultural dialogue across the Helsinki Metropolitan Area.

Mission & Strategic Priorities

Logrus ry is dedicated to developing an inclusive, open, and safe cultural platform where performing arts serve as a catalyst for social integration, mutual support, intergenerational dialogue, and community building in Finland.

  • Artistic Quality (Taiteellinen laatu): Producing and staging contemporary theatrical works in collaboration with professional directors, stage designers, playwrights, and performers.
  • Low-Threshold Accessibility (Matala kynnys & Saavutettavuus): Engaging diverse audiences regardless of social background, age, physical ability, or language proficiency.
  • Integration Through the Arts (Kotoutuminen taiteen keinoin): Empowering multicultural communities and bridging cultural ties in Finland through collaborative creative expression.
  • Safer Space Policy (Turvallisemman tilan periaatteet): Upholding rigorous standards of inclusion, respect, non-discrimination, and dedicated anti-harassment liaison support under our 2026–2028 plan.

Core Activities

  • Performing Arts & Theatre Production: Multilingual, physical, and visual stage performances with Finnish subtitling and universally accessible artistic language. Official theatre portal: teatterilogrus.fi.
  • Arts Education & Regular Workshops: Acting studios, stage speech, eco-fashion & sustainable costume design, dance/movement workshops, and Finnish language functional learning circles.
  • Partnership Programs: Collaborative productions and initiatives with metropolitan cultural centres (e.g., Caisa, Kanneltalo) and participation in cultural festivals across Finland.
