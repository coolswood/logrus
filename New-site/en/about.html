About Us

International Society for the Support of Youth Initiatives «LOGRUS ry» in Helsinki.

Our Mission

LOGRUS ry is a non-governmental organization created to bring young people together, cultivate creative abilities, foster intercultural integration, and support stage performance initiatives across Finland.

We produce cultural and theatrical shows, conduct regular skill workshops, run dance and art studios, and organize youth festivals and creative camps.

We actively collaborate with Finnish cultural centers and youth facilities (Caisa, Kanneltalo, Nuorisotilat) and offer certified workplace trial and internship programs through the TE-office.
